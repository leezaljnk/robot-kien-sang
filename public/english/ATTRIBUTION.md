# English Illustrations

The unmodified PNG illustrations in `images/illustrated/` are from Twemoji v16.0.1.

- Graphics: Twitter, Inc. and other contributors; maintained by the jdecked/twemoji contributors.
- Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/tree/v16.0.1/assets/72x72
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Re-download: `node scripts/download_english_illustrations.mjs` from the project root.

The original vocabulary and SVG files are preserved. The initial playable catalogue uses 28 illustrated school, animal, and food words. The other source words are not exposed as picture questions until suitable illustrations are available.
